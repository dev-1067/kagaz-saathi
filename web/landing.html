How it works

Four steps. Under ten seconds.

Indians lost over ₹20,000 crore to cyber fraud in 2025. Most scams work because the victim has nobody to ask. Kagzi is that somebody.

2

3

A plain explanation in Hindi, Marathi or English, read aloud, plus what to do and by when.

4

Act safely

One tap to report on 1930 or Chakshu, and alert a son or daughter.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can Kagzi be wrong?

Yes. It can miss a new kind of scam or call a real message suspicious. That is why it shows the reasons and always tells you to verify in the official app. If money was lost, call 1930 immediately.

Is my message stored?

Messages checked on this home page are not saved. Inside your account, checks are saved to your own history so you can review them, and you can delete any of them.

Does it read photos?

Yes. Upload a photo or screenshot and the text is read for you, on your own device. You can edit the text before checking.

Does the family alert really send an SMS?

No. Kagzi writes the alert and opens WhatsApp with it ready; you choose the chat and press send. A copy stays in your outbox.
